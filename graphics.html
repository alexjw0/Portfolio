Graphics & Videos

A collection of graphics and videos I have created for a variety of freelance, university and personal projects.

Great Gable

Utilising Adobe Illustrator, I designed and developed this poster for the Australian band, which was used across social media channels for their UK tour.

This was primarily on their Instagram platform, reaching an audience of over 20,000 followers.

This enhanced engagement and supported tour promotion efforts.

Grand Lotus

Utilised Photoshop for design and After Effects for animation to create an animated, looping Spotify Canvas for the band's newly released single.

Featuring on their Spotify profile, on the single itself and was even repurposed as promotional content across their social media channels.

Majak Door

Utilised Photoshop for design and After Effects for animation again to create an animated, looping Spotify Canvas for the band's album.
