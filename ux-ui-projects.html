Clozetto

Clozetto was my final-year university dissertation project, for which I achieved a First-Class mark and a summer scholarship from the university with funding and mentorship to develop the idea further. It is a campus-based student marketplace app designed to eliminate the environmental footprint and heavy transaction fees of platforms like Depop and Vinted. Through primary student surveys, competitor analysis, persona mapping, and iterative Figma prototyping, I created a peer-to-peer ecosystem enabling zero-fee, zero-shipping clothing trades directly on campus.

Cinemate

CineMate was a university design project created to combat the emotional fatigue, visual bias, and superficiality of traditional dating apps like Tinder, Hinge, and Bumble. Grounded in psychological literature reviews, competitor analysis, and embodied interaction theory, the project established a human-centered social and dating ecosystem. By replacing profile photos with iconic character avatars and introducing a single daily match alongside community meetup hubs, CineMate transforms digital interactions into meaningful, real-world connections driven by a shared love for cinema and television.
